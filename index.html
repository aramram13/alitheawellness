          Privacy & Data Policy

          Effective Date: June 2026

          At Alithea Wellness, we believe that the truth of your health belongs exclusively to you. This Privacy Statement outlines our strict commitment to data minimization, local storage, and your absolute sovereignty over your personal information.

          1. How Your Data is Stored (100% Local)

          Alithea Wellness operates as a strictly local application. We do not maintain, own, or lease any central databases, and we do not store your user profiles, health metrics, or daily logs on any external servers.

          • All information you provide is saved exclusively within your device's local browser storage.
          • Because your data never lives on our servers, we cannot sell, share, or monetize your personal health information.

          2. How Your Data is Processed (AI)

          To provide advanced features, the application must process your inputs using external Artificial Intelligence models.

          • When you request an AI calculation, the specific data required is securely transmitted to our processing endpoint.
          • This transmission is strictly ephemeral. It is used solely in real-time to generate feedback, after which it is returned to your device. We do not retain or log these transmissions.

          3. Data Security & Protection

          Because your data resides on your personal hardware, the security of your Alithea Wellness data relies on the security of your device. We recommend utilizing standard device-level security measures to protect access to your browser.

          4. Your Absolute Control

          • Granular Deletion: You may permanently delete any individual meal, workout, or vitals reading directly from your Dashboard.
          • Complete Erasure: You can permanently erase your entire profile and history by selecting the Delete All My Data button under your profile settings, or by clearing your browser’s cache for this application.
          • Data Portability: You may use the "Export Data" tool to download a complete JSON backup of your history.

          5. Medical Disclaimer

          Alithea Wellness is an informational tracking tool. It is not a medical device. The AI-generated feedback does not constitute professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider.
